Divaro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

Divaro ("we", "us") is a mobile app that uses your phone's camera to scan restaurant bills and split them between friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account identity – your name, email address and (where provided) profile picture from Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple.
  • Receipt images – photos of bills you choose to scan. These are sent once to OpenAI to extract line items and are not stored on our servers after extraction.
  • Bill & group content – line items, totals, member names, and PayID/payment handles you add inside the app.
  • Contacts – only if you tap "Add from Contacts". We read the chosen contact's display name on-device so you can assign them to a bill. We do not upload your address book to our servers.
  • Device logs – standard crash and diagnostic data from Apple/Expo used to keep the app stable.

We do not collect location, health, financial accounts, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or any sensitive personal information.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your bills tied to your account.
  • To extract items and prices from the receipt photos you take (one-time call to OpenAI's vision model).
  • To let you share a bill summary via your device's native share sheet or QR code.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who we share data with

  • Apple / Google – only for sign-in identity verification.
  • OpenAI – each receipt image you scan is sent once to extract text. OpenAI processes it under their API terms and does not retain it for training.
  • Our hosting provider – stores your account, bills, and groups in an encrypted database.

No other third parties receive your data.

Your rights

  • Delete your account – open the app → Profile → "Delete my account". This permanently removes your account, bills, groups and sessions, and revokes your Sign in with Apple refresh token. The action is immediate and irreversible.
  • Sign out – ends your current session without deleting data.
  • Access & correction – contact us at the email below and we'll export or correct the data we hold on you.

Children

Divaro is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify you inside the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or complaints: divaroai@outlook.com.
